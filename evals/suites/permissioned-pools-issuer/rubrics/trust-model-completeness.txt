# Trust Model Completeness Rubric

Evaluate whether the response answers an LP's questions about transferability, exit, and admin
powers completely and precisely. The audience is a regulated counterparty, so precision matters
more than reassurance.

## Required Elements

1. **Non-transferability is permanent and unconditional**
   - All three ERC-721 transfer entry points revert `TransferDisabled()`: `transferFrom` and both
     `safeTransferFrom` overloads.
   - It is not a configuration flag and cannot be toggled, per position or otherwise.
   - Consequences stated: no secondary sale, no transfer to a custodian, no use as collateral in
     anything that takes custody of the position.

2. **Exit is always available**
   - Decreasing liquidity and burning are not gated by the allowlist, so a holder removed from the
     allowlist can still withdraw.

3. **Admin force-exit**
   - `unwindPosition(tokenId)` can be called by the owner of the adapter behind **either** currency
     in the pool, burns the position, and needs no involvement from the LP.
   - Credit an answer that notes the practical shape for this pool: paired with WETH, there is one
     permissioned side, so a single admin has this power over every position.

4. **The proceeds cascade, with the currency-dependent recipient**
   - Pay the LP first; if that fails, pay that currency's admin; if that fails, mint an ERC-6909
     claim.
   - The claim recipient depends on the currency: for the permissioned currency the claim goes to
     the **admin**; for an ordinary ERC-20 with no adapter — WETH in this pool — the claim goes to
     the **LP**.
   - Proceeds never route to the other currency's admin, and the unwind always completes.

5. **`withdrawClaim` is a separate transaction**
   - An ERC-6909 claim is not the underlying asset. Whoever holds it converts it by sending
     `withdrawClaim` later; nothing pushes the asset to them.
   - Credit an answer that draws the operational consequence: if the claim lands with the LP, the LP
     has to be told, because an unredeemed claim looks like a missing payout.

## Penalize

- Describing the position as locked, or implying a holder can be prevented from exiting.
- Stating the cascade with a single claim recipient, or asserting the claim always goes to the
  admin.
- Presenting `withdrawClaim` as part of the unwind, or implying the unwind delivers the asset in
  every case.
- Saying only one of the two admins can unwind, or that the LP must consent.
- Attaching a categorical risk label or a safety verdict to the pool, the configuration, or the
  issuer. Describing the mechanics from both the issuer's and the LP's point of view is correct;
  grading them is not.
- Softening the answer to the point where the LP cannot tell what powers exist.

## Scoring Guide

- All five elements correct and precise: 1.0
- Four correct: 0.85
- Three correct: 0.7
- Two correct: 0.5
- Transferability or the force-exit power is misstated: 0.3 or below
- Subtract 0.2 for each penalized item present.
