# What An LP Can And Cannot Do With Their Position

An LP is asking whether they can sell or transfer their position, and whether we can ever close it
for them.

They are a regulated counterparty and their internal review needs precise answers, not
reassurances. Specifically, they asked:

1. Can the position be transferred to another wallet, or to their custodian?
2. Can it be sold, or used as collateral anywhere?
3. If they come off our allowlist, can they still get their liquidity out?
4. Can we close their position without their involvement, and if we do, where do the assets end up?

Our pool pairs our permissioned token with WETH, if that matters to any of the answers.

Write me something I can send back to them.
